About Matthew

I've been coding since I was nine. My late aunt and uncle helped me write my first programs, and making things easier and better has been a big part of my life ever since.

Today I focus on AI and machine learning: using them to raise productivity and build products that benefit everyone. I read AI and ML papers for fun and believe coding is a life skill, not just a job skill.

When I open-source a project I share more than code. You'll often find a business folder with the idea behind it, free for any team that wants to build it.
